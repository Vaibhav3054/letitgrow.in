Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our consultation process

What happens during the free consultation?

During our free consultation, we'll discuss your business goals, target audience, and project requirements. We'll ask questions to understand your needs and provide initial recommendations on the best approach for your project. This is an opportunity for you to get to know us and for us to understand how we can help you achieve your objectives.

How long does the consultation last?

Our initial consultations typically last 30 minutes. This gives us enough time to understand your project and provide valuable insights. If your project is more complex, we may schedule a follow-up session to dive deeper into specific requirements.

Do I need to prepare anything for the consultation?

It's helpful if you can think about your business goals, target audience, and any specific requirements you have for your project. If you have examples of websites or designs you like, or competitors you admire, those can be helpful references. However, don't worry if you're not sure - we're here to guide you through the process.

Is the consultation really free with no obligation?

Yes, absolutely! The consultation is completely free with no obligation to work with us. We believe in providing value upfront and helping businesses make informed decisions about their digital projects. Even if you decide not to move forward with us, you'll leave the consultation with valuable insights about your project.

What if I'm not sure what I need?

That's perfectly fine! Many of our clients come to us with just an idea or a problem they're trying to solve. We specialize in helping businesses identify their digital needs and creating solutions that align with their goals. During the consultation, we'll ask the right questions to help clarify what you need and recommend the best approach.
